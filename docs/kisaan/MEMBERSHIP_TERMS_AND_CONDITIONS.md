### 6. Renewal

6.1 **Auto-renewal is your choice.** When you buy a plan, you choose whether to turn auto-renewal on. It is **off unless you switch it on**.

6.2 If you turn auto-renewal on, you will set up a recurring payment mandate (UPI AutoPay or card) with your bank or app, which will ask you to authenticate. Your plan then renews at the end of each billing period at the price shown to you, unless you cancel.

6.3 Before each renewal:
- (a) **KISAAN** will remind you **at least 7 days before** the renewal date, with the price and date and how to cancel; and
- (b) **your bank or payment app** will send you a pre-debit notification **at least 24 hours before** the debit, with an option to skip that debit or cancel the mandate, as required by RBI rules.

6.4 If auto-renewal is off, your Membership ends at the end of the paid period. We will remind you before it ends, and you can renew manually.

6.5 If a renewal payment fails, your Membership enters a **grace period of `[3]` days**, during which benefits continue and we may retry the payment once your bank allows. If payment does not succeed within the grace period, your Membership ends.

### 8. Cancelling

8.1 You can cancel **at any time**, in the same place you joined: in the app (Membership → Cancel, in no more than two taps), or by telling the KISAAN assistant "cancel my membership" (it will confirm with you first), or through customer support.

8.3 We will not ask you to give a reason, and we will not make cancelling difficult. We may ask an optional feedback question.

8.4 You can also cancel the mandate directly in your bank/UPI app. Doing so stops renewal, but your Membership continues until the end of the paid period.

### B.2 Compliance checklist for counsel

1. **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020:** grievance officer display and timelines (48 h acknowledgement / one-month redressal); explicit affirmative consent (no pre-ticked boxes); full price breakup; disclosure of how to cancel regular payments; no cancellation charges unless the platform bears similar charges. Confirm whether B2B buyers buying for resale are "consumers" for Membership (likely yes for the Membership service itself).
2. **Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023:** subscription trap (no forced mandate for trial; easy cancellation); confirm shaming (neutral cancel copy); nagging (cap upsell frequency); false urgency (early-access countdowns must be real); drip pricing; basket sneaking (never pre-add Membership to a cart). The 5 June 2025 CCPA self-audit advisory: include Membership in the pre-launch self-audit.
3. **RBI E-mandate Framework 2026:** AFA at registration; ≤ ₹15,000 per debit without AFA; issuer pre-debit notice ≥24 h; customer can opt out of a debit or cancel; no charge for e-mandate use. Confirm the PA's implementation and the wording of clause 6.
4. **DPDP Act 2023 / Rules 2025:** notice content and language; consent for marketing separate from contract performance; retention; rights; phased commencement (main duties reported ~May 2027 — confirm live dates).
5. **GST:** membership fee likely a taxable service at 18%; invoice/credit-note handling; place of supply `[TAX REVIEW]`.
6. **Refund clause (9)** and **liability clause (13.3)** — enforceability and fairness under CPA 2019 (unfair contract terms).
7. **Language versions** — which version prevails; accuracy of the Punjabi/Hindi translations (professional legal translation required).
8. **Voice acceptance (1.4, 17.2)** — evidential sufficiency of voice confirmation plus app record; whether an in-app tap is also required. The proposed product rule: voice can initiate, but final acceptance is an on-screen tap or a recorded explicit verbal "yes" after the summary readback, stored with a transcript `[LEGAL REVIEW]`.
